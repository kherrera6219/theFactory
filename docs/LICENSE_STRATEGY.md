## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

Contributors must agree to [`CLA.md`](../CLA.md) when submitting a contribution.
The contributor retains copyright in their contribution while granting the
project maintainer the rights described in the CLA. This requirement does not
change the MIT terms under which the public project is distributed.

## Third-Party Licenses

theFactory depends on third-party packages such as FastAPI, Next.js, Redis,
PostgreSQL, and Qdrant. Their licenses apply independently. Dependency-license
and security checks are part of the repository's CI workflows.
